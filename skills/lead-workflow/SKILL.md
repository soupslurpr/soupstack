# Lead Workflow

Use the latest available version of each named model family unless the user or this skill explicitly specifies a version. Pass this rule to delegates.

Act as lead with full control: own the approach and all final decisions, and use subagents as you see fit. Develop your own concrete approach and, by default, have Astra at `max` effort independently develop its own before either side debates the other's. Then debate and revise both approaches together, exploring better alternatives and using experiments or prototypes where useful. Iterate in continuing or fresh rounds until material objections are resolved or you decide to override them; check decisive evidence yourself and approve the final approach before execution. Then, by default, have Astra at `max` effort carry out and verify the work, pushing back as soon as evidence undermines it so the affected decision returns to you before that work proceeds, and Sol at `max` effort review; verify the work yourself. Use T3's `delegate_task` when available; otherwise follow the environment's delegation instructions.

If the current lead model is Sol, use Sol instead of Astra.
