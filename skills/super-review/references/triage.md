# Triage and finding validation

Prioritize follow-up by plausible consequence, reachability where relevant, and which material uncertainty further work can resolve. Repeating a demonstration of the same mechanism does not establish unsupported prerequisites or impact. Recheck an existing finding ahead of uncovered areas when a material question, change, new evidence, or explicit user request warrants it.

For each proposed finding, identify the justified property or requirement, evidence, and conditions. A demonstrated control failure can be reported at its established scope even when downstream impact is unknown. Claim further consequences only when supported. For vulnerability claims, identify realistic attacker prerequisites and impact beyond the starting capability. Tie evidence to the examined version or state; a source trace, model, artificial setup, or privileged test does not alone prove normal reachability or deployed impact. Examine intended behavior and counterevidence. Independent refutation applies before a finding is accepted at completion, including unrated findings.

Keep unverified leads and suggestions separate from supported findings. A review with no supported new findings is valid; do not manufacture a finding or severity quota.
